Murky Browser

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what Murky Browser stores and how information is handled when you use the browser. Murky is designed so that ordinary browsing data stays on your device unless a feature or website you visit requires network communication.

1. Information stored locally

Murky may store settings, bookmarks, browsing history, recent searches, session information, and download preferences in your device's local application data. These records are used to provide browser features and are not intentionally uploaded to Murky servers.

2. Websites you visit

When you browse the web, your requests go to the websites and services you choose to visit. Those websites may collect information according to their own privacy policies. Murky does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

3. Search and voice features

Your selected search engine receives searches when you submit them. Murky's voice-search feature processes supported speech locally on your device when its local speech model is available. Network services used by a website you visit remain subject to that website's policies.

4. Blocking and Do Not Track

Murky can block requests to a built-in list of known advertising and tracking domains. You can also enable Do Not Track requests. Blocking is best-effort and does not guarantee that every advertisement or tracker will be prevented.

5. Downloads

Files you download are saved according to your browser settings and your operating system. Murky does not inspect the contents of downloaded files for advertising or analytics purposes.

6. Security

Murky uses Chromium's web isolation features for regular websites. No software can guarantee absolute security, so keep Murky and your operating system updated and use care when visiting unfamiliar sites or downloading files.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when Murky's features or data practices change. This section describes the current version of Murky.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about a particular Murky distribution, use the support or contact channel provided with that distribution.
